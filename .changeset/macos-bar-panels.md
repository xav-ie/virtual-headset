# The macOS panel matches your other status-bar panels

The macOS panel is built on [BarPanels](https://github.com/xav-ie/bar-panels), the library behind screenshare-ui's status-bar panels, and takes their look: Liquid Glass rows that animate as they change, checkmarks to pick the mic and the noise suppression model, switches for muting the call, noise suppression and keeping the mic open, and an Advanced section that opens in place. It keeps full keyboard control (arrows and Tab, Return, right and left to open and close Advanced, Escape), now closes when you click another item in the status bar, and stays out of screen shares like the other panels.
